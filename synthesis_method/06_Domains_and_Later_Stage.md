# Domain ideas and the later stage (Hawaiian becomes Tepenian)

**Status: `proposed` (developer's ideas, 2026-10-31; the timeline and names are canon, C-13 and C-14).** Source: the developer's note `to-be-integrated/components.txt` and the developer's answers the same day. The domain assignments are ideas, not decisions, and they do not fill the layering table (`02_Layering_Table.md`, C-7 Layering by register and domain) yet. The developer's rule for the empty slots: they are there for further registers, **but only to the extent that it makes sense within the language itself; nothing is forced.**

## The two stages (canon, C-13 Language timeline: Hawaiian to Tepenian)
1. **Hawaiian (Modern or Middle Hawaiian by vantage point), up to and through the Falkland Treaty (2564).** The pidgin-origin stage: English and Japanese primary, with Tagalog, Ilocano, Korean and Spanish (D-19 Build order: English and Japanese first).
2. **Tepenian, over the Second Interwar (2564 to 2812).** The language is influenced by many more languages and evolves into a unified language, the way English did.
3. **"Classical Tepenian"** is the corpus as written and spoken at the time of the Long Night War (2812). In later eras it is simply **"Solar"**, because everyone across the Solar System can understand it to some degree even if they cannot speak it (C-14 Names: Classical Tepenian and Solar).

Update 2026-11-03: Portuguese is a later-era language, because of Brazil's presence along and around the Palmer Peninsula (D-10 Portuguese: a later-era language); French is still to be checked. Weights at this stage, and whether languages bring grammar or only vocabulary, are not yet figured out.

## The cohesion goal (D-20 Cohesion: the language reads as one language)
The developer's aim for later-era Tepenian is the state English is in: its vocabulary comes from nearly every language English has ever drawn on, yet a sentence does not read as a mixture. The developer's test sentence:

> The paramedics who arrived at the scene of the truck accident logged the victim's cognitive abilities in their field notebook, whose deduction capacity proved compromised, and who evidently needed fast, quick, urgent physiological and possibly psychiatric care via hospitalization, and he needed it right then.

The developer's description: it contains words from almost every language English has been influenced by, and still looks like English. Which language each word comes from has **not been checked here**; it needs an etymological dictionary (rule 4, a recalled etymology is a guess). The goal for Tepenian: the contributions are **harmonized** so that it reads and feels like one cohesive language, not several languages in one. This also bears on rule 5 (the language is its own thing).
